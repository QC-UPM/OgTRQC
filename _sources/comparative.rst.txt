Comparative Analysis of Simulation Engines
==========================================

The comparative analysis of the data extracted from the generated reports reveals fundamental thermodynamic and informational differences between the phenomenological approximation and the strict quantum integrator. When observing the coupling trajectory between matter and geometry, the classical simulation shows a progressive and pronounced increase in the distortion of the octahedral cage, scaling from a base value of zero point eighteen to reach a maximum of zero point twenty-two at the end of the sixth simulation step. Conversely, the quantum engine, governed by von Neumann integration, subjects the system to a much more rigid and bounded structural evolution, where the distortion barely experiences marginal growth until stabilizing at zero point eighteen five under the same applied voltage profile. This divergence evidences that the exact tensorial treatment of the local Hamiltonian and trace preservation naturally limit the deformation of the crystal lattice, preventing the excessive volumetric deviation that the scalar proxy of the legacy model artificially allowed, which becomes evident when observing that the final volume displacement is almost an order of magnitude smaller in the quantum simulation.

By setting an instrumental resolution boundary condition of zero point zero two, the classical model quantified an atomic memory capacity of three point twenty-five. However, the execution of the quantum model raises this thermodynamic limit to three point eighty-five for that same threshold. This superior metric is systematically maintained throughout the entire resolution sweep, from an epsilon of zero point one to zero point zero zero two, indicating that the phase space defined by the exact coupling allows packing a greater amount of hidden equivalence classes of hydrogen polarons without them macroscopically overlapping. Consequently, the discussion of these results confirms that the transition towards a rigorous mathematical architecture not only improves the physical fidelity of the geometric relaxation trajectories but also brings to light a potential for non-Markovian memory retention in hydrogenated nickelate that is notably broader than postulated by empirical approximations.
