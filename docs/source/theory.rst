Generalized Time-Recoverable Quantum Control
--------------------------------------------
The framework of Generalized Time-Recoverable Quantum Control provides a closed-loop algebraic paradigm designed to model the non-Markovian feedback loops that occur between quantum matter states and localized structural crystal matrices. In this architectural suite, the host unit cell is mathematically modeled as an isolated octahedral geometry graph. The six vertices denote oxygen ligated positions defining the electronic field, while the central coordination position acts as the active Nickel electronic system center. Its orbital configuration, valence state, and polaronic correlations constitute the active non-volatile register.

.. math::

   \mathcal{H}_{cell} = \mathcal{H}_{Ni} \otimes \mathcal{H}_{O6} \otimes \ell^2(\Omega_H) \otimes \ell^2(\Omega_{oct})

Where the first omega defines the bounded configuration spaces of localized proton-polaron interstitial anomalies and the second omega represents the tensor space of discrete octahedral lattice states.

Kolmogorov-Tikhomirov Metric Entropy Bounds
-------------------------------------------
The third strong point brought by this development is the application of Kolmogorov-Tikhomirov entropy to quantify storage capacity. Typically, the capacity of a material memory is evaluated by counting discrete and statically distinguishable resistance states. The proposed simulation poses a profound paradigm shift by measuring capacity through the analysis of the number of equivalence classes of hidden configurations that are currently indistinguishable, but will produce divergent responses in the future above a given instrumental resolution threshold.

.. math::

   C_{atom}(\epsilon) = \log_2(N_\epsilon)

Where N denotes the total available subset of stable spatial configurations of hydrogen polarons whose future memory readback signals are separated by a minimum Euclidean distance dictated by the resolution boundary.
